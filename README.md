# Beatrice Mensah

Beatrice Mensah is a Senior Lecturer in Data Science at the School of Computing & Informatics. She joined Ridgemont Institute of Technology in January 2026 and teaches the core course DS-401 (Applied Machine Learning) for the 2026 intake.

She holds a Ph.D. in Data Science (2018) from Ridgemont Institute of Technology and an M.Sc. in Applied Statistics (2013) from the University of Cape Coast. Before joining the institute, she was a Lecturer at the University of Cape Coast from 2019 to 2025.

Her research focuses on fairness-aware machine learning and algorithmic bias in public-sector decision systems. In 2022 she received the Early Career Research Award from the Ghana Statistical Association.
